Building a more accessible explorer for the TON blockchain.

We created Tonscan to support a fast-growing TON ecosystem as it scaled beyond early adopters.

As TON gained traction, its tools struggled to keep up. The challenge was to make complex, low-level blockchain data intuitive, without losing the depth required by more advanced users.

We approached Tonscan as a system rather than a flow, shaping a flexible structure that supports multiple intents, from quick lookups to deeper exploration.

Under the hood, a custom indexer and data pipeline process millions of daily transactions in real time, turning raw blockchain data into structured, queryable information.

The result is a fast, scalable explorer that makes TON easier to understand, navigate, and trust, helping open the ecosystem to a broader audience.
