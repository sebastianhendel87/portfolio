Project Secondary Layer Tool
2021
Work Production, delivery, cross-team coordination
Team Design, Engineering, Product (Stink + Spotify)

Helping Spotify scale internal tools
for cross-team collaboration

The brief was to build an internal tool that could generate bespoke secondary-layer assets from each content photo, tailored to the image, not a one-size-fits-all overlay.

It had to be sexy and functional: strong visually, straightforward to use, and self-explanatory for third parties and vendors so handoffs stayed simple.

We started from wireframes and a light direction (explore a few gradients, try a pattern) and iterated into something shippable. The result was a polished, practical product, delivered on time.
