Dune

Visual Identity + Website 2025 · Project management

An evolution of Dune’s brand for its next phase: clearer positioning, a cohesive system across product and web, and motion that feels refined rather than noisy, so the company could move fast without losing what made the mark recognizable.

1.0

The challenge

Dune had strong brand recognition, but its identity was starting to feel out of sync with where the product and the company were heading.

In a crypto landscape dominated by neon and visual noise, the opportunity was to move in a more refined, distinctive direction: modernizing without replacing what people already knew.

2.0

The process

We audited what carried equity and what limited it, then built a more cohesive system around those core elements, defining how new products like SIM could live inside the same ecosystem.

3.0

The outcome

A refreshed brand system and a growing product ecosystem: SIM as a connected sub-brand, and a website that explains the offer, the stack, and where Dune is headed.

4.0

Results & motion

The updated brand gave Dune a more distinctive, scalable presence: clearer navigation for users and a stronger story for launches and partnerships.
