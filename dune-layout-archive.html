  • dune-v2.html

Local preview

From the portfolio folder in Terminal:

python3 serve.py

Then open, for example:

http://localhost:8877/dune-layout-archive.html (port may differ)

Five clips (dune-01 … dune-05) are loaded from your Mac via serve.py — see serve.py for the Documents/…/Dune path. Hero logo-reveal-dark.mp4 stays in the repo dune/ folder.
